# CMR Hospitality Suite
## AI-Powered Hotel Management Platform for African Hospitality Businesses

---

### The Problem

Most Nigerian hotels are still managing operations with:
- WhatsApp groups for reservations
- Excel sheets for guest records  
- Manual cash tracking for payments
- No visibility into occupancy or revenue
- No way to track staff or housekeeping

This leads to:
- Lost bookings
- Revenue leakage
- Poor guest experience
- No business intelligence

---

### The Solution

CMR Hospitality Suite is a complete cloud-based hotel management platform built specifically for African hospitality businesses.

**One platform. All your operations.**

- Reservations & Check-in/Check-out
- Guest Management & CRM
- Room Management
- Payment Processing (Paystack)
- Staff & Housekeeping
- Event Hall Management
- AI Concierge (powered by Claude AI)
- Real-time Analytics
- Multi-property support
