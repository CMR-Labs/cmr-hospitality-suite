### Step 1 — Dashboard (3 minutes)
Show:
- Live KPIs (occupancy rate, revenue, guests, reservations)
- Room status breakdown
- Quick actions

Say:
"The moment you log in, you see the health of your entire hotel.
No spreadsheets. No WhatsApp groups. Everything in one place."

---

### Step 2 — Rooms (3 minutes)
Show:
- Room grid with statuses
- Available vs Occupied vs Cleaning
- Update a room status live

Say:
"Every room is tracked in real time.
Your front desk always knows what's available."

### Step 3 — New Reservation (4 minutes)
Show:
- Click + New Reservation
- Select guest, room, dates
- Show payment status
- Show Pay via Paystack button

Say:
"Creating a reservation takes 30 seconds.
Guests can pay by card, bank transfer, or USSD — all through Paystack."

Show:

- Total stays, total spend

"You build a complete profile of every guest.

### Common Objections

**"We already use Excel"**
"Excel doesn't send payment confirmations, track occupancy,
or let you ask an AI about your business at 2am."

**"Is my data safe?"**
"Your data is stored on Supabase — enterprise-grade PostgreSQL
with encryption at rest and in transit. Each hotel's data is
completely isolated."

**"What if we have internet issues?"**
"The system works on any device with internet.
For offline scenarios, we're building a mobile app."

**"Is it too expensive?"**
"₦31,083/month is ₦1,036/day.
What does one lost booking cost you?"
