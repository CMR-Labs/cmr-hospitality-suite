# CMR Hospitality Suite Roadmap

> **Status:** Active Development (Release Candidate)
>
> CMR Hospitality Suite is an AI-powered, multi-tenant hospitality management platform built to help hotels, resorts, guest houses, and event centers streamline operations through automation, analytics, and intelligent business tools.

---

# Vision

Build Africa's leading hospitality operations platform by combining modern cloud infrastructure, automation, artificial intelligence, and enterprise-grade security into a single SaaS solution.

# Long-Term Vision

CMR Hospitality Suite aims to become a comprehensive hospitality operating system that enables businesses to manage reservations, guests, payments, staff, analytics, customer relationships, and AI-powered services from a unified platform.

By combining enterprise-grade security, cloud-native architecture, intelligent automation, and scalable SaaS infrastructure, the platform is designed to support organizations ranging from boutique guest houses to large multi-property hotel groups.
